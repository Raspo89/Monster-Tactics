# Reference observations used for V 7

User-supplied Combat Monsters 1.mp 4 (~13 m 16) and Combat Monsters 2.mp 4 (~19 m 27) were reviewed via chronological 20-second storyboards and selected detailed frames in the preceding work. Only these 2 of 19 videos are covered here.

- Video 1 ~03:50–04:00: victory/coin animation then acquired-card dialog. The amount observed was animated; it does not establish a fixed original win payout or drop probabilities.
- Video 1 ~05:15: abilities appear as compact horizontal tiles below the board, with a basic attack and a separately costed special ability.
- Video 1 ~10:40: Russ physical card enlarged at right. Blue frame denotes Mage; rarity C remains a separate top-corner indicator. Fireball has its own Mojo cost; Feasting is a separate race ability.
- Video 1 ~12:59 and Video 2 opening: parchment open-book menus against a fiery red hex floor. Solo Quests is a map/node progression mode and is not equivalent to a handful of skirmish stages.
- Video 2 ~02:40–09:20: compact corner HUD, bottom hand and ability tiles; no large combat sidebars.
- Video 2 ~10:00 onward: round gold/stone battle platform with hex engraving. Other shown arenas use different layouts. Native layouts have varying masks, so the previous 9 x 7 rectangle is not an original universal map.

Rarity help recovered from native locale establishes Common,Uncommon,Rare,Legendary. Unique relates to collection counts, not an additional rarity tier. V 7's 70/23/6/1 drop split and 60/180/600/1800 prices are custom balance values stated in UI and README.
