# Version 6 — active monster abilities

Adds 92 real native monster records with their actual named abilities, original summoning cost, power, health, rarity, archetype and skin. All 53 special ability types assigned in the original monster catalogue are represented. Four additional basic-attack definitions are retained in the 57-entry ability table. Existing 128 AT_NONE monsters remain unchanged, and the total playable collection is 272 cards.

The engine now exposes an authenticated-by-state monsterAbility action: owner/current turn, living caster, attack availability, affordability, correct target/team/range and legal tile are checked even on the AI fast path. Casting pays the ability's Mojo cost, independently of summoning, and spends the attack token even for free/support abilities. Newly summoned monsters may cast. No resource advantage is granted to AI.

Source mechanics implemented: additive attack power; direct/area damage; healing; Mojo generation; card draw; buffs and debuffs; unique permanent Mighty/Berserk; stacking permanent Take Aim; flame with Imp racial upkeep; timed Daze/Snare/IceBlock counted on target-owned upkeeps; robot immunity; shielding; cure; regeneration; vengeance on offensive abilities; push; teleport; movement restoration; disarm; Fire/Trap/Barrier tiles. Ability range is hex distance without the prototype basic-attack rock line-of-sight rule. IceBlocked creatures cannot act or be targeted and take no damage. Traps hit both teams. Fire entry spends defence and causes Flame; Imps ignore its initial hit.

Validation: 91 previous engine/ability/source/rune/projection tests passed; all 15 race/catalogue tests passed, including full normal-AI matches for each of 16 races using the updated native presets. The new monster-ability suite exercises every special ability's activation, resource payment and one-use limit, plus targeted damage/affordability/ownership/team/range checks, status durations, immunity, regeneration, burning, resource-loop prevention, AI ability use, teleport/Sprint, vengeance, and fire/trap interactions. Final suite count: 70 new tests, 176 distinct tests across seven suites.

Browser QA: opened the interactive ability tutorial, selected Duron, saw its native summoning cost 5 and separate ability cost 1, activated Savage Beating, selected the highlighted adjacent enemy, observed Mojo fall 3→2, enemy die and ability button become disabled. The larger left ability panel remains usable while the card inspection panel appears on the right. Native portraits and fallback animations rendered. WebGL is unavailable in the cloud browser, so GPU rendering remains visually unverified. Direct desktop file-open behavior of the self-contained HTML is statically checked rather than browser-tested here.

Boundaries: the full 1600-monster catalogue, original spell/equipment set and original game modes remain incomplete. Runtime uses the prototype arena topology and effect animations. Native effect costs/parameters and audited rules are recovered; this is not a claim of binary-identical simulation in every edge-case or original visual effects. Evidence is in reference/native-rules/ACTIVE-ABILITIES-AUDIT-V6.md; asset conversion provenance is in reference/conversion/ABILITY-ASSETS-V6.md.
